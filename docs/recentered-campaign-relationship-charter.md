# Sonoma County Campaign & Consultant Relationship Charter

## Project Mission
The Sonoma County Political Relationship & Influence Database is an evidence-centered civic research platform designed to map the financial, professional, and organizational networks that power local campaigns and political actions.

Our primary focus is the relationships among:
* **Campaigns** & **Candidates**
* **Political Consultants** & **Campaign Managers**
* **Public-Affairs Firms** & **Lobbying Firms**
* **Campaign Vendors** & **Subcontractors**
* **Donors** (Individuals, Businesses, PACs, Unions)
* **Businesses**, **Industry Groups**, and **Trade Associations**
* **Nonprofits** & **Labor Unions**

## Supporting Evidence vs. Primary Focus
* **Primary Focus**: Direct transactional and relationship linkages between political campaigns, consultants, vendors, donors, and businesses.
* **Supporting Evidence**: Government records, public agency meetings, agendas, and roll-call votes are secondary supporting sources. They are ingested *only* when they directly document a campaign, consulting, lobbying, or organizational relationship. They must never serve as the primary organizing structure of the application.

## Principles of Representation
1. **Evidence-Centered**: Every relationship edge in the system must connect to an official public record source or transaction ID.
2. **Non-Judgmental Directive**: The database documents relationships, not wrongdoing. The system must not conclude that a documented relationship proves improper influence, corruption, coordination, or conflict of interest.
3. **Transparency of Confidence**: All relationships must be clearly labeled as `VERIFIED` (reviewed by a researcher), `PROVISIONAL` (auto-matched by the pipeline), `DISPUTED` (under active investigation), or `UNVERIFIED`.
