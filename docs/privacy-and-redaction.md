# Privacy and Redaction Policy

## Redaction Principles
The database exists to record public political and financial activities. Irrelevant private data disclosed in public filings must be protected:
- **Redacted Fields**: Bank account numbers, signatures, personal telephone numbers, personal email addresses, and home addresses.
- **Official Disambiguation**: Street and city addresses from official filings are retained in staging solely for disambiguating entities with identical names, but are suppressed in public-facing views.
