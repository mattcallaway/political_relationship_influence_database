# Project Charter: Sonoma County Political Relationship & Influence Database

## Mission Statement
The Sonoma County Political Relationship & Influence Database is an evidence-centered civic-research platform designed to organize public records and document political, financial, governmental, professional, business, and civic relationships. 

Its core purpose is to help researchers, journalists, and the public understand how people, organizations, campaigns, committees, projects, contracts, lobbying activities, appointments, government decisions, payments, meetings, votes, and public records connect over time.

---

## Core Tenets & Product Pillars

### 1. Evidence-Backed Provenance
No node or edge exists in isolation. The platform preserves the chain of custody from raw source documents to researcher interpretations.
* **Original Sources**: Preservation of raw uploaded documents.
* **Granular Provenance**: Page- and region-level coordinates mapping where data originated.
* **Extraction Audits**: Retaining raw machine extractions alongside researcher corrections.

### 2. Research-Centered, Tool-Agnostic
Automatic parsing, OCR processing, interactive timelines, and network graphs are supporting tools. They are not the mission. The mission is researcher utility, verification, and clear analysis.

### 3. Preserving Uncertainty & Conflict
Civic research is messy and full of contradictions. The database does not enforce artificial consensus. It preserves:
* **Uncertainty & Conflicting Evidence**: Support for competing claims and contradictory sources.
* **Status Distinctions**: Clear separation of machine-derived, provisional, reviewed, verified, disputed, and rejected records.
* **Audited Revisions**: Logged merge history and correction trails.

---

## The Non-Judgmental Directive

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The existence of a documented relationship, contribution, expenditure, contract, appointment, shared affiliation, meeting, vote, or chronological overlap **does not by itself demonstrate improper influence, coordination, corruption, conflict of interest, or wrongdoing.**
> The platform is built to document facts with source evidence. Normative interpretation remains the sole responsibility of the human researcher. The system must never auto-generate pejorative labels.
