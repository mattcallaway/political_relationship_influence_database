Research Methodology & Platform Integrity

The Sonoma County Political Relationship & Influence Database is an evidence-centered civic research platform. Our purpose is to help researchers and the public map connections over time without imposing pre-emptive or pejorative conclusions.

Core Editorial Principles

  • 1. Strict Evidence Provenance

    Every single contribution, expenditure, public contract, lobbying activity, and board appointment must be tied to an official public record source (e.g. California FPPC Form 460 filings, official meeting minutes, county contractor registries). No unsourced claims are permitted.

  • 2. Non-Pejorative Stance

    All connections represent associations backed by evidence. The database documents relationships, not impropriety. We do not characterize campaign finance flows, lobbying targeting, or contracts with subjective labels of wrongdoing or conflict of interest.

  • 3. Entity Deduplication and Provisional Matching

    When importing records, candidates, vendors, and donors are matched using strict confidence scores. Automatic mergers are only performed when confidence exceeds 95%. Matches with lower scores remain provisional and require manual approval in the review queue.

Connection Definitions

Campaign Finance (Form 460 Schedule A & E)

Maps donors contributing money to campaign committees and committees making expenditures to campaign vendors or consultants. All values are itemized or unitemized totals as declared on California FPPC disclosures.

Lobbying Disclosures

Maps representation contracts where a registered lobbying firm advocates on behalf of a client entity targeting specific government agencies.

Board & Commission Appointments

Tracks appointments of individuals to county boards, planning commissions, and municipal seats, linking the appointee, the appointer, and the term duration.
